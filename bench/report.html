What the runs showed

Speed is solved. Groq still wins it.

gpt-oss-120b answers in 93 ms at the median and reasons a little before answering. The Cerebras build of the same model is 160 ms. Most of the top tier answers in about 150 ms. GPT-5.6 Luna takes 0.3 s and Gemini 3.8 Flash 1.2 s.

A little thinking beats a bigger model.

Same Llama 70B, same hardware: your 2024 prompt (two guesses with explanations, then pick one) scored 30.3. The bare “just the year” prompt scored 15.5. The models that win all reason briefly before answering.

The ceiling is Wikidata, not the models.

On 122 of 1,036 cards, all four top models miss by more than 25 years, usually with the same textbook answer. The Sikh Empire is “founded” in 1733 in Wikidata, but every model says 1799. The House of Plantagenet is 1126 in Wikidata versus the 1154 everyone learns.

Free options exist, with catches.

Qwen3.8 27B on your 4090 places 92.7% of early cards at 441 ms, free. Gemini Flash-Lite on your Google key’s free tier is about as accurate as Opus but limited to roughly 35 calls a minute. Gemma 3 1B is too small and gets 1 in 12 years exactly right.

Subscriptions aren’t the way.

The Claude Code and Codex CLIs give good answers, but every card pays 2–5 s of agent start-up. The whole 22-setup benchmark cost $3.57 in OpenRouter credit. A game with gpt-oss costs about two-tenths of a cent.

Sonnet 5.5 can’t stop explaining.

It ignored “reply with only the year” on 565 of 1,036 cards and wrote a sentence first. Once those replies were parsed properly it was right up with the leaders. Haiku 5.5 declined to date 16 biblical figures.

Run it

The app is on branch modernize-2026 of your fork. The model picker on the start screen holds the best setups above. Add ?model=gptoss120-groq&autostart&pace=600 to the URL to record a game at watchable speed.

cp .env.example .env.local     # OPENROUTER_API_KEY, GOOGLE_API_KEY…
npm install && npm run dev
cd bench && node run.ts all && node report.ts   # rerun the benchmark
